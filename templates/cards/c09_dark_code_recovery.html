-- Find recoverable dropped volumes
SHOW VOLUMES DROPPED IN my_catalog.my_schema;

-- Restore using the returned volume ID
UNDROP VOLUME WITH ID 'your-volume-id';
7-day recovery window
  • Lists dropped volumes that are still recoverable
  • Restore with UNDROP VOLUME (with ID) within 7 days
  • Works for both managed & external volumes
  • Managed volume files are also recovered
